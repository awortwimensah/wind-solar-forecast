    Optimal day-ahead offer

    Revenue for offer q and production p: R(q) = λDq + λ↓(p−q)+ − λ↑(q−p)+. Maximising the expected revenue over the forecast distribution gives the newsvendor solution: offer the τ* = (λD−λ↓)/(λ↑−λ↓) quantile. Quantile forecasts (pinball loss) are therefore the right tool, not the MSE-optimal mean.

    Battery scheduling with dynamic programming

    State = state of charge (SoC), action = charge / idle / discharge, reward = price × energy − degradation. The optimal schedule is found by backward induction of the Bellman equation Vt(s) = maxa [ rt(s,a) + Vt+1(s′) ] over the selected period.

    Site assessment and representative days

    A full year of ERA5 reanalysis is converted to hourly capacity factors. Each day becomes a 48-value vector (24 h wind + 24 h solar) and k-means groups them into representative days, each weighted by the number of days it represents — the input format of capacity-expansion models.

    Methods and data

    Data sources (all free, no API key)

    • Open-Meteo — Forecast API (≤16 days), Historical Forecast API (archived forecasts), Ensemble API (ECMWF ENS, GEFS, ICON-EPS), ERA5 archive. CC BY 4.0, non-commercial use.
    • Energi Data Service (Energinet) — Nordic/German day-ahead prices (DK1, DK2, DE, NO, SE). CC BY 4.0.
    • BigDataCloud — client-side reverse geocoding of your GPS position. OpenStreetMap map tiles.

    Physical models

    • Hub-height wind: hourly power-law shear fitted between 10 m and 100 m; air density from surface pressure and temperature; IEC density-corrected power curve.
    • PV: solar position at mid-interval, Erbs decomposition, isotropic transposition, Faiman cell temperature, PVWatts DC model, inverter clipping.
    • Hourly products: energy in hour [h, h+1) uses the radiation mean ending at h+1 and the mean of instantaneous wind at h and h+1.

    Course methods used (DTU 46765)

    • Regression, regularisation & validation — ridge regression with polynomial features; λ and degree chosen by blocked k-fold CV (no shuffling of time series); ridge keeps XᵀX + λI invertible even with redundant features.
    • Error metrics — MAE (median-optimal, robust) and RMSE (mean-optimal, penalises large errors) shown side by side, normalised by capacity.
    • Quantile forecasting — ensemble quantiles P10/P50/P90, pinball loss for evaluation.
    • Electricity markets — two-price balancing revenue R(q); optimal offer is the τ* quantile.
    • Decision-focused learning — linear offer q = β₀ + β₁x trained by maximising realised revenue (projected subgradient ascent), compared with OLS.
    • Clustering — k-means++ on daily wind/solar profiles; weighted representative days; elbow plot; duration-curve check (chronology between days is lost).
    • MDPs — finite-horizon backward induction for battery scheduling; value and policy iteration for the Markov-price battery exercise.

    Forecasts are indicative only. Weather data by Open-Meteo.com, prices from Energinet (www.energidataservice.dk).
