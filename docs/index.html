    Combination forecast

    Weather models are good in different situations. The weight of each model is learned from its past error separately for each weather situation (wind: low wind / power-curve slope / high wind; solar: clear / broken / overcast) using weights ∝ (1/MSE)p, where the sharpness p is chosen on held-out data, and the spread of past errors in that situation gives a situation-dependent uncertainty band. The latest measurement then corrects the first hours, with the error decaying as e−Δt/τ.

    Optimal day-ahead offer

    Revenue for offer q and production p: R(q) = λDq + λ↓(p−q)+ − λ↑(q−p)+. Maximising the expected revenue over the forecast distribution gives the newsvendor solution: offer the τ* = (λD−λ↓)/(λ↑−λ↓) quantile. Quantile forecasts (pinball loss) are therefore the right tool, not the MSE-optimal mean.

    Battery scheduling with dynamic programming

    State = state of charge (SoC), action = charge / idle / discharge, reward = price × energy − degradation. The optimal schedule is found by backward induction of the Bellman equation Vt(s) = maxa [ rt(s,a) + Vt+1(s′) ] over the selected period.

    Evaluate forecasts against your measurements and learn a better model

    Upload hourly plant data (CSV with time in UTC and power_mw, or wind_power_mw/solar_power_mw). The app downloads the archived forecasts for the same hours and compares: raw physical model → ridge regression with polynomial features (λ and degree chosen by blocked cross-validation) → decision-focused learning (linear offer trained to maximise revenue, not accuracy).

    Site assessment and representative days

    A full year of ERA5 reanalysis is converted to hourly capacity factors. Each day becomes a 48-value vector (24 h wind + 24 h solar) and k-means groups them into representative days, each weighted by the number of days it represents — the input format of capacity-expansion models.
